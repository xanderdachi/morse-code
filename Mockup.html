About Morse Club

Pip carries your transmissions. He has opinions about your timing.

A typing trainer where the drill is real writing. Every level is a passage that someone thought worth keeping — a line of Hamlet, a koan, a proverb from Ghana, the opening formula of a Russian tale. You send it in Morse, and you get it back graded.

Nothing is marked wrong while you are sending. Operators do not get corrected mid-message, and neither do you.

No accounts. No sign-up. Progress lives in this browser, and the weekly chart only ever gets a run you explicitly post.

All passages are in the public domain or oral tradition. Attribution is shown with each one.
